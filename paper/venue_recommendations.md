## Recommendation

For the current project, target the next IEEE BigData cycle after the revised model comparison and real latency results are available. Consider ICDM only if the evaluation reveals a substantial, generalizable finding and the novelty is sharpened. Consider IEEE Blockchain only after adding a well-defined ledger threat model and measuring the audit subsystem; otherwise the blockchain element is an integration feature rather than a research contribution.

The current paper draft uses IEEE conference formatting but is not submission-ready. Its results are intentionally pending. Remove author identities for any double- or triple-blind submission and use the target year's official template and page rules.
